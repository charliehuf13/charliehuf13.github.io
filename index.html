Welcome

I am Charles Hough, a bioinformatics researcher completing a B.S. in Biology at Oregon State University (Spring 2026) with a minor in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology. My research combines computational genomics with wet-lab microbiology to answer biological questions at both sequence and systems levels.

I currently develop and implement comparative genomics workflows in the Chang Lab and design PCR-based microbial identification workflows in the Halsey Lab. In Fall 2026, I will continue this trajectory in the M.S. Bioinformatics program at Georgia Tech.

Research Areas: Genomics, HPC workflows, microbial systems, and computational biology.

CURRENT FOCUS

  • Scaling comparative genomics pipelines on HPC systems.
  • Molecular identification of microbial consortia using PCR.
  • Applying machine learning to biological datasets (in progress).
