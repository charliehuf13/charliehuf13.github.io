Academic Trajectory

I am completing a B.S. in Biology at Oregon State University (Spring 2026) with a minor in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, and I will begin an M.S. in Bioinformatics at Georgia Tech in Fall 2026.

Research Profile

My work bridges computational genomics and molecular biology. I develop and scale genomic analysis pipelines on HPC systems while also designing and executing wet-lab workflows for microbial identification and consortium studies.

Dual Research Expertise

Computational Genomics (Chang Lab)

  • Developed comparative genomics workflows for 100+ genomes using GENESPACE, MUMmer, and nf-core pipelines.
  • Implemented genome assembly quality assessments with BUSCO and downstream annotation validation.
  • Designed SLURM-based HPC workflows with Singularity containers for reproducible, large-scale analysis.

Molecular Biology & Microbial Systems (Halsey Lab)

  • Designed and ran a PCR-based 16S rRNA molecular identification workflow from DNA extraction through sequencing.
  • Executed DNA extraction, PCR amplification, and gel electrophoresis for microbial organism identification.
  • Contributed to biofuel-focused consortium experiments involving two algal species and Bacillus pumilus, including contamination sequencing and organism identification.
